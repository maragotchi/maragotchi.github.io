★ Flashing Your Meets

If you can do a puzzle, you can flash a meets.

★ What Is This?

We all know Tamagotchi's and if you're here, you know the meets. Let me preface this: yes, it's possible to fry your device. If you double check yourself, triple check it will NOT happen.

I am, however, NOT responsible for your mistakes. There are pictures, there are gifs. You CAN do this... just take your time and you'll do fine!

★ Tools Needed

⚙

Hardware

  • Tamagotchi Meets/ON
  • SPI flash programmer (CH347)
  • SOIC8 test clip
  • Small Phillips screwdriver
⌨

Software

  • AsProgrammer
  • ROM file. Don't look at me. I don't have one.
★

Prep

  • Patience & steady hands
  • Clean, well-lit workspace
  • Original ROM backup!
  • Maybe some cups for the screws?
